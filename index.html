Research Interests

Three questions drive my work

My path runs from mechanical engineering to psychology, and each of these questions needs both.

01

Human Factors & Motion Sickness

Why do VR and automated driving make people sick? I predict susceptibility from DTI-derived brain networks and study how it differs between adults and children.

02

Human–Technology Interaction

How do interface design — predictability cues, non-driving tasks, feedback — shape comfort and performance inside VR, vehicles, and robotic systems?

03

Engineering Methods for Human Research

Experiments need apparatus. I build the VR environments, six-axis platforms, and data pipelines that psychological studies run on.
